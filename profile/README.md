# Omuk

Free, private apps for Mac and Android, made by one developer. Each app does one job well.
No ads, no account, no analytics.

**[omuk.dev](https://omuk.dev)**

| App | Platform | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [airshot](https://omuk.dev/airshot/) | Mac | Screenshots, GIFs, screen recordings and text from the screen (OCR). |
| [Flume](https://omuk.dev/flume/) | Mac | A download manager, like IDM for Windows. Fast, and resumes after a break. |
| [AirBridge](https://omuk.dev/airbridge/) | Mac and Android | AirDrop between a Mac and an Android phone, over your own Wi-Fi. |
| [Klipboard](https://omuk.dev/klipboard/) | Mac | A clipboard manager that also finds words inside copied images. |
| [PostVault](https://omuk.dev/postvault/) | Android | Saves posts from TikTok, YouTube and Instagram on your phone. |
| [Gallery](https://omuk.dev/gallery/) | Android | A private photo gallery. Nothing leaves the phone. Coming soon. |

Download the apps from [omuk.dev](https://omuk.dev). Found a bug? Use the form at [omuk.dev/support](https://omuk.dev/support/).
