## How they're made

**Your data stays yours.** Captures stay on your Mac. Files go straight across your Wi-Fi, never through a server.

**One job each.** Every app solves one problem, and lives where you already work: the menu bar, the share sheet, a keystroke.

**Free.** No ads, no trials, no account. Download and use them.
